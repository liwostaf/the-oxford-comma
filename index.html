Our Story and Mission

Creating a third space on Brandeis' campus

Founded by an international student, aiming to fill three voids on our campus:

1. Operating hours gap

The existing campus vendors aren't open early enough, particularly on weekends when students are working events with an 8am call time and nothing is open until 9:30; there's also nothing open late enough where night owls can enjoy something fresh and warm that's not a cup noodle from the convenience store, and delivery services are expensive and add up fast. The goal of expanding from a pop-up to a coffee cart will allow for this to exist.

2. Not enough variety

Whilst we do have three cafés on campus already, they all serve poorly made beverages that look and taste the same, with very few customization options. With a team of trained baristas, and high-quality beans and matcha, we're aiming to offer the best service and create beverages that are worth your money.

3. Absence of a true third space

Brandeis currently lacks a relaxed, unhurried space where students don't feel pressured or compelled to be productive or rush out the door. At the Comma, you can stay for a chat, meet the people you've been trying to catch and hang out with but can never find a time that aligns, and enjoy a proper break from the pressures of class and work.

International Student Employment Priority

Brandeis is home to an extraordinarily diverse international student body. However, visa rules strictly limit off-campus employment. The Oxford Comma prioritizes hiring International students on campus to provide meaningful, flexible income and professional growth opportunities.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our pop-ups, hours, loyalty cards, and hiring.

Where will the pop-up be located first?

Our initial launch pop-up will take place in the Blue Booths. Following the pop-up testing phase, we will transition into a mobile cart moving between peak foot traffic spots on campus.

What are the operating hours?

When active, the cart operates daily from 7:00 AM to 11:00 PM, explicitly designed to serve early risers and late-night study sessions.

How do physical loyalty stamp cards work?

We issue physical stamped loyalty cards at our cart! Buy 8 drinks and get your 9th drink completely free. We chose physical cards to encourage social interaction at the cart.

Why are International students prioritized for hiring?

Federal visa regulations restrict International students from working off campus. By prioritizing International student applicants for on-campus employment at our coffee cart, we help build an inclusive, supportive campus environment.
